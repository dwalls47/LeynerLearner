1:1 The burden which Habakkuk the prophet saw.
1:2 How long, O HaShem, should I cry, and You will not hear? I cry out to You of violence, and You will not save.
1:3 Why do You show me iniquity, and beholdest mischief? And why are spoiling and violence before me? so that there is strife, and contention arises.
1:4 Therefore the law is slacked, and right never goes out; for the wicked beset the righteous; therefore right goes out perverted.
1:5 Look among the nations, and and wonder marvellously; for, a work will be wrought in your days, which you will not believe though it be told you.
1:6 For, I raise up the Chaldeans, that bitter and impetuous nation, that march through the breadth of the earth, to possess dwelling-places that are not theirs.
1:7 They are terrible and dreadful; their law and their majesty proceed from themselves.
1:8 Their horses also are swifter than leopards, and are more fierce than the wolves of the desert; and their horsemen spread themselves; indeed, their horsemen come from far, they fly as a vulture that hasts to devour.
1:9 They come all of them for violence; their faces are set eagerly as the east wind; and they gather captives as the sand.
1:10 And they scoff at kings, and princes are a derision to them; they deride every stronghold, for they heap up earth, and take it.
1:11 Then their spirit passes over and transgress, and they become guilty: even they who impute their might to their god.
1:12 Art not You from everlasting, O HaShem my G-d, my Holy One? We will not die. O HaShem, You have ordained them for judgment, and You, O Rock, have established them for correction.
1:13 You that art of eyes too pure to see evil, and that can not look on mischief, therefore look You, when they deal treacherously, and holdest Your peace, when the wicked swallows up the man that is more righteous than he;
1:14 And make men as the fishes of the sea, as the creeping things, that have no ruler over them?
1:15 They take up all of them with the angle, they catch them in their net, and gather them in their drag; therefore they rejoice and exult.
1:16 Therefore they sacrifice to their net, and offer to their drag; because by them their portion is fat, and their food plenteous.
1:17 Will they therefore empty their net, and not spare to kill the nations continually?
2:1 I will stand on my watch, and set me on the tower, and will look out to see what He will speak by me, and what I will answer when I am reproved.
2:2 And HaShem answered me, and said: 'Write the vision, and make it plain on tables, that a man may read it swiftly.
2:3 For the vision is yet for the appointed time, and it declares of the end, and does not lie; though it tarry, wait for it; because it will surely come, it will not delay.'
2:4 Look, his soul is puffed up, it is not upright in him; but the righteous will live by his faith.
2:5 Indeed, moreover, wine is a treacherous dealer; the haughty man does not abide; he who enlarges his desire as the nether-world, and is as death, and cannot be satisfied, but gathers to him all nations, and heaps to him all peoples.
2:6 Will not all these take up a parable against him, and a taunting riddle against him, and say: 'Woe to him that increases that which is not his! how long? and that lades himself with many pledges!'
2:7 Will they not rise up suddenly that will exact interest of you, and awake that will violently shake you, and you shall be for booties to them?
2:8 Because you have spoiled many nations, all the remnant of the peoples will spoil you; because of men's blood, and for the violence done to the land, to the city and to all that live in it.
2:9 Woe to him that gains evil gains for his house, that he may set his nest on high, that he may be delivered from the power of evil!
2:10 You have devised shame to your house, by cutting off many peoples, and have forfeited your life.
2:11 For the stone will cry out of the wall, and the beam out of the timber will answer it.
2:12 Woe to him that builds a town with blood, and establishes a city by iniquity!
2:13 Look, is it not of HaShem of hosts that the peoples labor for the fire, and the nations weary themselves for vanity?
2:14 For the earth will be filled with the knowledge of the glory of HaShem, as the waters cover the sea.
2:15 Woe to him that gives his neighbor drink, that put your venom to it, and make him drunken also, that you may look on their nakedness!
2:16 You are filled with shame instead of glory, drink also, and be uncovered; the cup of HaShem's right hand will be turned to you, and filthiness will be on your glory.
2:17 For the violence done to Lebanon will cover you, and the destruction of the beasts, which made them afraid; because of men's blood, and for the violence done to the land, to the city and to all that live in it.
2:18 What profits the graven image, that the maker of it has graven it, even the molten image, and the teacher of lies; that the maker of his work trusts in it, to make dumb idols?
2:19 Woe to him that says to the wood: 'Awake', to the dumb stone: 'Arise!' Can this teach? Look, it is overlaid with gold and silver, and there is no breath at all in the middle of it.
2:20 But HaShem is in His holy temple; let all the earth keep silence before Him.
3:1 A prayer of Habakkuk the prophet. On Shigionoth.
3:2 O HaShem, I have heard the report of You, and am afraid; O HaShem, revive Your work in the middle of the years, in the middle of the years make it known; in wrath remember compassion.
3:3 G-d comes from Teman, and the Holy One from mount Paran. Selah. His glory covers the heavens, and the earth is full of His praise.
3:4 And a brightness appears as the light; rays has He at His side; and there is the hiding of His power.
3:5 Before him goes the pestilence, and fiery bolts go out at His feet.
3:6 He stands, and shakes the earth, He sees, and makes the nations to tremble; and the everlasting mountains are dashed in pieces, the ancient hills bow; His goings are as of old.
3:7 I see the tents of Cushan in affliction; the curtains of the land of Midian tremble.
3:8 Is it, O HaShem, that against the rivers, is it that Your anger is kindled against the rivers, or Your wrath against the sea? that You ride on Your horses, on Your chariots of victory?
3:9 Your bow is made quite bare; sworn are the rods of the word. Selah. You cleave the earth with rivers.
3:10 The mountains have seen You, and they tremble; the tempest of waters flows over; the deep utters its voice, and lifts up its hands on high.
3:11 The sun and moon stand still in their habitation; at the light of Your arrows as they go, at the shining of Your glittering spear.
3:12 You march through the earth in indignation, You thresh the nations in anger.
3:13 You have come out for the deliverance of Your people, for the deliverance of Your anointed; You wound the head out of the house of the wicked, uncovering the foundation even to the neck. Selah
3:14 You have stricken through with his own rods the head of his rulers, that come as a whirlwind to scatter me; whose rejoicing is as to devour the poor secretly.
3:15 You have trodden the sea with Your horses, the foaming of mighty waters.
3:16 When I heard, my inward parts trembled, my lips quivered at the voice; rottenness enters into my bones, and I tremble where I stand; that I should wait for the day of trouble, when he comes up against the people that he invades.
3:17 For though the fig-tree will not blossom, neither will fruit be in the vines; the labor of the olive will fail, and the fields will yield no food; the flock will be cut off from the fold, and there will be no herd in the stalls;
3:18 Yet I will rejoice in HaShem, I will exult in the G-d of my salvation.
3:19 G-d, the Lord, is my strength, and He makes my feet like hinds' feet, and He makes me to walk on my high places. For the Leader. With my string-music.
