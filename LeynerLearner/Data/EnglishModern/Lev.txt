1:1 HaShem called to Moses, and spoke to him out of the tent of meeting, saying:
1:2 Speak to the children of Israel, and say to them: When any man of you brings an offering to HaShem, you shall bring your offering of the cattle, even of the herd or of the flock.
1:3 If his offering be a burnt-offering of the herd, he will offer it a male without blemish; he will bring it to the door of the tent of meeting, that he may be accepted before HaShem.
1:4 And he will lay his hand on the head of the burnt-offering; and it will be accepted for him to make atonement for him.
1:5 And he will kill the bullock before HaShem; and Aaron's sons, the priests, will present the blood, and dash the blood round about against the altar that is at the door of the tent of meeting.
1:6 And he will flay the burnt-offering, and cut it into its pieces.
1:7 And the sons of Aaron the priest will put fire on the altar, and lay wood in order on the fire.
1:8 And Aaron's sons, the priests, will lay the pieces, and the head, and the suet, in order on the wood that is on the fire which is on the altar;
1:9 but its inwards and its legs will he wash with water; and the priest will make the whole smoke on the altar, for a burnt-offering, an offering made by fire, of a sweet savor to HaShem.
1:10 And if his offering be of the flock, whether of the sheep, or of the goats, for a burnt-offering, he will offer it a male without blemish.
1:11 And he will kill it on the side of the altar northward before HaShem; and Aaron's sons, the priests, will dash its blood against the altar round about.
1:12 And he will cut it into its pieces; and the priest will lay them, with its head and its suet, in order on the wood that is on the fire which is on the altar.
1:13 But the inwards and the legs will he wash with water; and the priest will offer the whole, and make it smoke on the altar; it is a burnt-offering, an offering made by fire, of a sweet savor to HaShem.
1:14 And if his offering to HaShem be a burnt-offering of fowls, then he will bring his offering of turtle-doves, or of young pigeons.
1:15 And the priest will bring it to the altar, and pinch off its head, and make it smoke on the altar; and the blood of it will be drained out on the side of the altar.
1:16 And he will take away its crop with the feathers of it, and cast it beside the altar on the east part, in the place of the ashes.
1:17 And he will rend it by the wings of it, but will not divide it asunder; and the priest will make it smoke on the altar, on the wood that is on the fire; it is a burnt-offering, an offering made by fire, of a sweet savor to HaShem.
2:1 And when any one brings a meal-offering to HaShem, his offering will be of fine flour; and he will pour oil on it, and put frankincense on it.
2:2 And he will bring it to Aaron's sons the priests; and he will take thereout his handful of the fine flour of it, and of the oil of it, together with all the frankincense of it; and the priest will make the memorial-part of it smoke on the altar, an offering made by fire, of a sweet savor to HaShem.
2:3 But that which is left of the meal-offering will be Aaron's and his sons'; it is a thing most holy of the offerings of HaShem made by fire.
2:4 And when you bring a meal-offering baked in the oven, it will be unleavened cakes of fine flour mingled with oil, or unleavened wafers spread with oil.
2:5 And if your offering be a meal-offering baked on a griddle, it will be of fine flour unleavened, mingled with oil.
2:6 You shall break it in pieces, and pour oil on it; it is a meal-offering.
2:7 And if your offering be a meal-offering of the stewing-pan, it will be made of fine flour with oil.
2:8 And you shall bring the meal-offering that is made of these things to HaShem; and it will be presented to the priest, and he will bring it to the altar.
2:9 And the priest will take off from the meal-offering the memorial-part of it, and will make it smoke on the altar — an offering made by fire, of a sweet savor to HaShem.
2:10 But that which is left of the meal-offering will be Aaron's and his sons'; it is a thing most holy of the offerings of HaShem made by fire.
2:11 No meal-offering, which you shall bring to HaShem, will be made with leaven; for you shall make no leaven, nor any honey, smoke as an offering made by fire to HaShem.
2:12 As an offering of first-fruits you may bring them to HaShem; but they will not come up for a sweet savor on the altar.
2:13 And every meal-offering of yours shall you season with salt; neither shall you suffer the salt of the covenant of your G-d to be lacking from your meal-offering; with all your offerings you shall offer salt.
2:14 And if you bring a meal-offering of first-fruits to HaShem, you shall bring for the meal-offering of your first-fruits grain in the ear parched with fire, even groats of the fresh ear.
2:15 And you shall put oil on it, and lay frankincense on it; it is a meal-offering.
2:16 And the priest will make the memorial-part of it smoke, even of the groats of it, and of the oil of it, with all the frankincense of it; it is an offering made by fire to HaShem.
3:1 And if his offering be a sacrifice of peace-offerings: if he offer of the herd, whether male or female, he will offer it without blemish before HaShem.
3:2 And he will lay his hand on the head of his offering, and kill it at the door of the tent of meeting; and Aaron's sons the priests will dash the blood against the altar round about.
3:3 And he will present of the sacrifice of peace-offerings an offering made by fire to HaShem: the fat that covers the inwards, and all the fat that is on the inwards,
3:4 and the two kidneys, and the fat that is on them, which is by the loins, and the lobe above the liver, which he will take away hard by the kidneys.
3:5 And Aaron's sons will make it smoke on the altar on the burnt-offering, which is on the wood that is on the fire; it is an offering made by fire, of a sweet savor to HaShem.
3:6 And if his offering for a sacrifice of peace-offerings to HaShem be of the flock, male or female, he will offer it without blemish.
3:7 If he bring a lamb for his offering, then will he present it before HaShem.
3:8 And he will lay his hand on the head of his offering, and kill it before the tent of meeting; and Aaron's sons will dash the blood of it against the altar round about.
3:9 And he will present of the sacrifice of peace-offerings an offering made by fire to HaShem: the fat of it, the fat tail entire, which he will take away hard by the rump-bone; and the fat that covers the inwards, and all the fat that is on the inwards,
3:10 and the two kidneys, and the fat that is on them, which is by the loins, and the lobe above the liver, which he will take away by the kidneys.
3:11 And the priest will make it smoke on the altar; it is the food of the offering made by fire to HaShem.
3:12 And if his offering be a goat, then he will present it before HaShem.
3:13 And he will lay his hand on the head of it, and kill it before the tent of meeting; and the sons of Aaron will dash the blood of it against the altar round about.
3:14 And he will present of it his offering, even an offering made by fire to HaShem: the fat that covers the inwards, and all the fat that is on the inwards,
3:15 and the two kidneys, and the fat that is on them, which is by the loins, and the lobe above the liver, which he will take away by the kidneys.
3:16 And the priest will make them smoke on the altar; it is the food of the offering made by fire, for a sweet savor; all the fat is HaShem's.
3:17 It will be a perpetual statute throughout your generations in all your homes, that you shall eat neither fat nor blood.
4:1 And HaShem spoke to Moses, saying:
4:2 Speak to the children of Israel, saying: If any one will sin through error, in any of the things which HaShem has not commanded to be done, and will do any one of them:
4:3 if the anointed priest will sin so as to bring guilt on the people, then let him offer for his sin, which he has sinned, a young bullock without blemish to HaShem for a sin-offering.
4:4 And he will bring the bullock to the door of the tent of meeting before HaShem; and he will lay his hand on the head of the bullock, and kill the bullock before HaShem.
4:5 And the anointed priest will take of the blood of the bullock, and bring it to the tent of meeting.
4:6 And the priest will dip his finger in the blood, and sprinkle of the blood seven times before HaShem, in front of the veil of the sanctuary.
4:7 And the priest will put of the blood on the horns of the altar of sweet incense before HaShem, which is in the tent of meeting; and all the remaining blood of the bullock will he pour out at the base of the altar of burnt-offering, which is at the door of the tent of meeting.
4:8 And all the fat of the bullock of the sin-offering he will take off from it; the fat that covers the inwards, and all the fat that is on the inwards,
4:9 and the two kidneys, and the fat that is on them, which is by the loins, and the lobe above the liver, which he will take away by the kidneys,
4:10 as it is taken off from the ox of the sacrifice of peace-offerings; and the priest will make them smoke on the altar of burnt-offering.
4:11 But the skin of the bullock, and all its flesh, with its head, and with its legs, and its inwards, and its dung,
4:12 even the whole bullock will he carry out without the camp to a clean place, where the ashes are poured out, and burn it on wood with fire; where the ashes are poured out will it be burnt.
4:13 And if the whole congregation of Israel will err, the thing being hid from the eyes of the assembly, and do any of the things which HaShem has not commanded to be done, and are guilty:
4:14 when the sin in which they have sinned is known, then the assembly will offer a young bullock for a sin-offering, and bring it before the tent of meeting.
4:15 And the elders of the congregation will lay their hands on the head of the bullock before HaShem; and the bullock will be killed before HaShem.
4:16 And the anointed priest will bring of the blood of the bullock to the tent of meeting.
4:17 And the priest will dip his finger in the blood, and sprinkle it seven times before HaShem, in front of the veil.
4:18 And he will put of the blood on the horns of the altar which is before HaShem, that is in the tent of meeting, and all the remaining blood will he pour out at the base of the altar of burnt-offering, which is at the door of the tent of meeting.
4:19 And all the fat of it will he take off from it, and make it smoke on the altar.
4:20 Thus will he do with the bullock; as he did with the bullock of the sin-offering, so will he do with this; and the priest will make atonement for them, and they will be forgiven.
4:21 And he will carry out the bullock without the camp, and burn it as he burned the first bullock; it is the sin-offering for the assembly.
4:22 When a ruler sins, and does through error any one of all the things which HaShem his G-d has not commanded to be done, and is guilty:
4:23 if his sin, in which he has sinned, be known to him, he will bring for his offering a goat, a male without blemish.
4:24 And he will lay his hand on the head of the goat, and kill it in the place where they kill the burnt-offering before HaShem; it is a sin-offering.
4:25 And the priest will take of the blood of the sin-offering with his finger, and put it on the horns of the altar of burnt-offering, and the remaining blood of it will he pour out at the base of the altar of burnt-offering.
4:26 And all the fat of it will he make smoke on the altar, as the fat of the sacrifice of peace-offerings; and the priest will make atonement for him as concerning his sin, and he will be forgiven.
4:27 And if any one of the common people sin through error, in doing any of the things which HaShem has not commanded to be done, and be guilty:
4:28 if his sin, which he has sinned, be known to him, then he will bring for his offering a goat, a female without blemish, for his sin which he has sinned.
4:29 And he will lay his hand on the head of the sin-offering, and kill the sin-offering in the place of burnt-offering.
4:30 And the priest will take of the blood of it with his finger, and put it on the horns of the altar of burnt-offering, and all the remaining blood of it will he pour out at the base of the altar.
4:31 And all the fat of it will he take away, as the fat is taken away from off the sacrifice of peace-offerings; and the priest will make it smoke on the altar for a sweet savor to HaShem; and the priest will make atonement for him, and he will be forgiven.
4:32 And if he bring a lamb as his offering for a sin-offering, he will bring it a female without blemish.
4:33 And he will lay his hand on the head of the sin-offering, and kill it for a sin-offering in the place where they kill the burnt-offering.
4:34 And the priest will take of the blood of the sin-offering with his finger, and put it on the horns of the altar of burnt-offering, and all the remaining blood of it will he pour out at the base of the altar.
4:35 And all the fat of it will he take away, as the fat of the lamb is taken away from the sacrifice of peace-offerings; and the priest will make them smoke on the altar, on the offerings of HaShem made by fire; and the priest will make atonement for him as touching his sin that he has sinned, and he will be forgiven.
5:1 And if any one sin, in that he hears the voice of adjuration, he being a witness, whether he has seen or known, if he do not utter it, then he will bear his iniquity;
5:2 or if any one touch any unclean thing, whether it be the carcass of an unclean beast, or the carcass of unclean cattle, or the carcass of unclean swarming things, and be guilty, it being hidden from him that he is unclean;
5:3 or if he touch the uncleanness of man, whatever his uncleanness be with which he is unclean, and it be hid from him; and, when he knows of it, be guilty;
5:4 or if any one swear clearly with his lips to do evil, or to do good, whatever it be that a man will utter clearly with an oath, and it be hid from him; and, when he knows of it, be guilty in one of these things;
5:5 and it will be, when he will be guilty in one of these things, that he will confess that in which he has sinned;
5:6 and he will bring his forfeit to HaShem for his sin which he has sinned, a female from the flock, a lamb or a goat, for a sin-offering; and the priest will make atonement for him as concerning his sin.
5:7 And if his means do not suffice for a lamb, then he will bring his forfeit for that in which he has sinned, two turtle-doves, or two young pigeons, to HaShem: one for a sin-offering, and the other for a burnt-offering.
5:8 And he will bring them to the priest, who will offer that which is for the sin-offering first, and pinch off its head close by its neck, but will not divide it asunder.
5:9 And he will sprinkle of the blood of the sin-offering on the side of the altar; and the rest of the blood will be drained out at the base of the altar; it is a sin-offering.
5:10 And he will prepare the second for a burnt-offering, according to the ordinance; and the priest will make atonement for him as concerning his sin which he has sinned, and he will be forgiven.
5:11 But if his means do not suffice for two turtledoves, or two young pigeons, then he will bring his offering for that in which he has sinned, the tenth part of an ephah of fine flour for a sin-offering; he will put no oil on it, neither will he put any frankincense on it; for it is a sin-offering.
5:12 And he will bring it to the priest, and the priest will take his handful of it as the memorial-part of it, and make it smoke on the altar, on the offerings of HaShem made by fire; it is a sin-offering.
5:13 And the priest will make atonement for him as touching his sin that he has sinned in any of these things, and he will be forgiven; and the remnant will be the priest's, as the meal-offering.
5:14 And HaShem spoke to Moses, saying:
5:15 If any one commit a trespass, and sin through error, in the holy things of HaShem, then he will bring his forfeit to HaShem, a ram without blemish out of the flock, according to your valuation in silver by shekels, after the shekel of the sanctuary, for a guilt-offering.
5:16 And he will make restitution for that which he has done amiss in the holy thing, and will add the fifth part to it, and give it to the priest; and the priest will make atonement for him with the ram of the guilt-offering, and he will be forgiven.
5:17 And if any one sin, and do any of the things which HaShem has not commanded to be done, though he do not know it, yet is he guilty, and will bear his iniquity.
5:18 And he will bring a ram without blemish out of the flock, according to your valuation, for a guilt-offering, to the priest; and the priest will make atonement for him concerning the error which he committed, though he did not know it, and he will be forgiven.
5:19 It is a guilt-offering — he is certainly guilty before HaShem.
5:20 And HaShem spoke to Moses, saying:
5:21 If any one sin, and commit a trespass against HaShem, and deal falsely with his neighbor in a matter of deposit, or of pledge, or of robbery, or have oppressed his neighbor;
5:22 or have found that which was lost, and deal falsely in it, and swear to a lie; in any of all these that a man does, sinning in it;
5:23 then it will be, if he has sinned, and is guilty, that he will restore that which he took by robbery, or the thing which he has gotten by oppression, or the deposit which was deposited with him, or the lost thing which he found,
5:24 or any thing about which he has sworn falsely, he will even restore it in full, and will add the fifth part more to it; to him to whom it appertains will he give it, in the day of his being guilty.
5:25 And he will bring his forfeit to HaShem, a ram without blemish out of the flock, according to your valuation, for a guilt-offering, to the priest.
5:26 And the priest will make atonement for him before HaShem, and he will be forgiven, concerning whatever he does so as to be guilty by it.
6:1 And HaShem spoke to Moses, saying:
6:2 Command Aaron and his sons, saying: This is the law of the burnt-offering: it is that which goes up on its firewood on the altar all night to the morning; and the fire of the altar will be kept burning by it.
6:3 And the priest will put on his linen garment, and his linen breeches will he put on his flesh; and he will take up the ashes whereto the fire has consumed the burnt-offering on the altar, and he will put them beside the altar.
6:4 And he will put off his garments, and put on other garments, and carry out the ashes without the camp to a clean place.
6:5 And the fire on the altar will be kept burning by it, it will not go out; and the priest will kindle wood on it every morning; and he will lay the burnt-offering in order on it, and will make smoke on it the fat of the peace-offerings.
6:6 Fire will be kept burning on the altar continually; it will not go out.
6:7 And this is the law of the meal-offering: the sons of Aaron will offer it before HaShem, in front of the altar.
6:8 And he will take up therefrom his handful, of the fine flour of the meal-offering, and of the oil of it, and all the frankincense which is on the meal-offering, and will make the memorial-part of it smoke on the altar for a sweet savor to HaShem.
6:9 And that which is left of it will Aaron and his sons eat; it will be eaten without leaven in a holy place; in the court of the tent of meeting they will eat it.
6:10 It will not be baked with leaven. I have given it as their portion of My offerings made by fire; it is most holy, as the sin-offering, and as the guilt-offering.
6:11 Every male among the children of Aaron may eat of it, as a due for ever throughout your generations, from the offerings of HaShem made by fire; whatever touches them will be holy.
6:12 And HaShem spoke to Moses, saying:
6:13 This is the offering of Aaron and of his sons, which they will offer to HaShem in the day when he is anointed: the tenth part of an ephah of fine flour for a meal-offering perpetually, half of it in the morning, and half of it in the evening.
6:14 On a griddle it will be made with oil; when it is soaked, you shall bring it in; in broken pieces shall you offer the meal-offering for a sweet savor to HaShem.
6:15 And the anointed priest that will be in his place from among his sons will offer it, it is a due for ever; it will be wholly made to smoke to HaShem.
6:16 And every meal-offering of the priest will be wholly made to smoke; it will not be eaten.
6:17 And HaShem spoke to Moses, saying:
6:18 Speak to Aaron and to his sons, saying: This is the law of the sin-offering: in the place where the burnt-offering is killed will the sin-offering be killed before HaShem; it is most holy.
6:19 The priest that offers it for sin will eat it; in a holy place will it be eaten, in the court of the tent of meeting.
6:20 Whatever will touch the flesh of it will be holy; and when there is sprinkled of the blood of it on any garment, you shall wash that on which it was sprinkled in a holy place.
6:21 But the earthen vessel in which it is sodden will be broken; and if it be sodden in a brazen vessel, it will be scoured, and rinsed in water.
6:22 Every male among the priests may eat of it; it is most holy.
6:23 And no sin-offering, of which any of the blood is brought into the tent of meeting to make atonement in the holy place, will be eaten; it will be burnt with fire.
7:1 And this is the law of the guilt-offering: it is most holy.
7:2 In the place where they kill the burnt-offering will they kill the guilt-offering: and the blood of it will be dashed against the altar round about.
7:3 And he will offer of it all the fat of it: the fat tail, and the fat that covers the inwards,
7:4 and the two kidneys, and the fat that is on them, which is by the loins, and the lobe above the liver, which he will take away by the kidneys.
7:5 And the priest will make them smoke on the altar for an offering made by fire to HaShem; it is a guilt-offering.
7:6 Every male among the priests may eat of it; it will be eaten in a holy place; it is most holy.
7:7 As is the sin-offering, so is the guilt-offering; there is one law for them; the priest that makes atonement with it, he will have it.
7:8 And the priest that offers any man's burnt-offering, even the priest will have to himself the skin of the burnt-offering which he has offered.
7:9 And every meal-offering that is baked in the oven, and all that is dressed in the stewing-pan, and on the griddle, will be the priest's that offers it.
7:10 And every meal-offering, mingled with oil, or dry, will all the sons of Aaron have, one as well as another.
7:11 And this is the law of the sacrifice of peace-offerings, which one may offer to HaShem.
7:12 If he offer it for a thanksgiving, then he will offer with the sacrifice of thanksgiving unleavened cakes mingled with oil, and unleavened wafers spread with oil, and cakes mingled with oil, of fine flour soaked.
7:13 With cakes of leavened bread he will present his offering with the sacrifice of his peace-offerings for thanksgiving.
7:14 And of it he will present one out of each offering for a gift to HaShem; it will be the priest's that dashes the blood of the peace-offerings against the altar.
7:15 And the flesh of the sacrifice of his peace-offerings for thanksgiving will be eaten on the day of his offering; he will not leave any of it until the morning.
7:16 But if the sacrifice of his offering be a vow, or a freewill-offering, it will be eaten on the day that he offers his sacrifice; and on the morrow that which remains of it may be eaten.
7:17 But that which remains of the flesh of the sacrifice on the third day will be burnt with fire.
7:18 And if any of the flesh of the sacrifice of his peace-offerings be at all eaten on the third day, it will not be accepted, neither will it be imputed to him that offers it; it will be an abhorred thing, and the soul that eats of it will bear his iniquity.
7:19 And the flesh that touches any unclean thing will not be eaten; it will be burnt with fire. And as for the flesh, every one that is clean may eat of it.
7:20 But the soul that eats of the flesh of the sacrifice of peace-offerings, that pertain to HaShem, having his uncleanness on him, that soul will be cut off from his people.
7:21 And when any one will touch any unclean thing, whether it be the uncleanness of man, or an unclean beast, or any unclean detestable thing, and eat of the flesh of the sacrifice of peace-offerings, which pertain to HaShem, that soul will be cut off from his people.
7:22 And HaShem spoke to Moses, saying:
7:23 Speak to the children of Israel, saying: You shall eat no fat, of ox, or sheep, or goat.
7:24 And the fat of that which dieth of itself, and the fat of that which is torn of beasts, may be used for any other service; but you shall in no wise eat of it.
7:25 For whoever eats the fat of the beast, of which men present an offering made by fire to HaShem, even the soul that eats it will be cut off from his people.
7:26 And you shall eat no manner of blood, whether it be of fowl or of beast, in any of your homes.
7:27 Whoever it be that eats any blood, that soul will be cut off from his people.
7:28 And HaShem spoke to Moses, saying:
7:29 Speak to the children of Israel, saying: He that offers his sacrifice of peace-offerings to HaShem will bring his offering to HaShem out of his sacrifice of peace-offerings.
7:30 His own hands will bring the offerings of HaShem made by fire: the fat with the breast will he bring, that the breast may be waved for a wave-offering before HaShem.
7:31 And the priest will make the fat smoke on the altar; but the breast will be Aaron's and his sons'.
7:32 And the right thigh shall you give to the priest for a heave-offering out of your sacrifices of peace-offerings.
7:33 He among the sons of Aaron, that offers the blood of the peace-offerings, and the fat, will have the right thigh for a portion.
7:34 For the breast of waving and the thigh of heaving have I taken of the children of Israel out of their sacrifices of peace-offerings, and have given them to Aaron the priest and to his sons as a due for ever from the children of Israel.
7:35 This is the consecrated portion of Aaron, and the consecrated portion of his sons, out of the offerings of HaShem made by fire, in the day when they were presented to minister to HaShem in the priest's office;
7:36 which HaShem commanded to be given them of the children of Israel, in the day that they were anointed. It is a due for ever throughout their generations.
7:37 This is the law of the burnt-offering, of the meal-offering, and of the sin-offering, and of the guilt-offering, and of the consecration-offering, and of the sacrifice of peace-offerings;
7:38 which HaShem commanded Moses in mount Sinai, in the day that he commanded the children of Israel to present their offerings to HaShem, in the wilderness of Sinai.
8:1 And HaShem spoke to Moses, saying:
8:2 'Take Aaron and his sons with him, and the garments, and the anointing oil, and the bullock of the sin-offering, and the two rams, and the basket of unleavened bread;
8:3 and assemble all the congregation at the door of the tent of meeting.'
8:4 And Moses did as HaShem commanded him; and the congregation was assembled at the door of the tent of meeting.
8:5 And Moses said to the congregation: 'This is the thing which HaShem has commanded to be done.'
8:6 And Moses brought Aaron and his sons, and washed them with water.
8:7 And he put on him the tunic, and girded him with the girdle, and clothed him with the robe, and put the ephod on him, and he girded him with the skilfully woven band of the ephod, and bound it to him with it.
8:8 And he placed the breastplate on him; and in the breastplate he put the Urim and the Thummim.
8:9 And he set the mitre on his head; and on the mitre, in front, did he set the golden plate, the holy crown; as HaShem commanded Moses.
8:10 And Moses took the anointing oil, and anointed the tabernacle and all that was in it, and sanctified them.
8:11 And he sprinkled of it on the altar seven times, and anointed the altar and all its vessels, and the laver and its base, to sanctify them.
8:12 And he poured of the anointing oil on Aaron's head, and anointed him, to sanctify him.
8:13 And Moses brought Aaron's sons, and clothed them with tunics, and girded them with girdles, and bound head-tires on them; as HaShem commanded Moses.
8:14 And the bullock of the sin-offering was brought; and Aaron and his sons laid their hands on the head of the bullock of the sin-offering.
8:15 And when it was killed, Moses took the blood, and put it on the horns of the altar round about with his finger, and purified the altar, and poured out the remaining blood at the base of the altar, and sanctified it, to make atonement for it.
8:16 And he took all the fat that was on the inwards, and the lobe of the liver, and the two kidneys, and their fat, and Moses made it smoke on the altar.
8:17 But the bullock, and its skin, and its flesh, and its dung, were burnt with fire without the camp; as HaShem commanded Moses.

11:1 And HaShem spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying to them:
11:2 Speak to the children of Israel, saying: These are the living things which you may eat among all the beasts that are on the earth.
11:3 Whatever parts the hoof, and is wholly cloven-footed, and chews the cud, among the beasts, that may you eat.
11:4 Nevertheless these shall you not eat of them that only chew the cud, or of them that only part the hoof: the camel, because he chews the cud but does not part the hoof, he is unclean to you.
11:5 And the rock-badger, because he chews the cud but does not part the hoof, he is unclean to you.
11:6 And the hare, because she chews the cud but does not part the hoof, she is unclean to you.
11:7 And the swine, because he parts the hoof, and is cloven-footed, but does not chew the cud, he is unclean to you.
11:8 Of their flesh you shall not eat, and their carcasses you shall not touch; they are unclean to you.
11:9 These may you eat of all that are in the waters: whatever has fins and scales in the waters, in the seas, and in the rivers, them may you eat.
11:10 And all that have not fins and scales in the seas, and in the rivers, of all that swarm in the waters, and of all the living creatures that are in the waters, they are a detestable thing to you,
11:11 and they will be a detestable thing to you; you shall not eat of their flesh, and their carcasses you shall have in detestation.
11:12 Whatever has no fins nor scales in the waters, that is a detestable thing to you.
11:13 And these you shall have in detestation among the fowls; they will not be eaten, they are a detestable thing: the great vulture, and the bearded vulture, and the ospray;
11:14 and the kite, and the falcon after its kinds;
11:15 every raven after its kinds;
11:16 and the ostrich, and the night-hawk, and the sea-mew, and the hawk after its kinds;
11:17 and the little owl, and the cormorant, and the great owl;
11:18 and the horned owl, and the pelican, and the carrion-vulture;
11:19 and the stork, and the heron after its kinds, and the hoopoe, and the bat.
11:20 All winged swarming things that go on all fours are a detestable thing to you.
11:21 Yet these may you eat of all winged swarming things that go on all fours, which have jointed legs above their feet, with which to leap on the earth;
11:22 even these of them you may eat: the locust after its kinds, and the bald locust after its kinds, and the cricket after its kinds, and the grasshopper after its kinds.
11:23 But all winged swarming things, which have four feet, are a detestable thing to you.
11:24 And by these you shall become unclean; whoever touches the carcass of them will be unclean until even.
11:25 And whoever bears anything of the carcass of them will wash his clothes, and be unclean until the even.
11:26 Every beast which parts the hoof, but is not cloven footed, nor chews the cud, is unclean to you; every one that touches them will be unclean.
11:27 And whatever goes on its paws, among all beasts that go on all fours, they are unclean to you; whoever touches their carcass will be unclean until the even.
11:28 And he that bears the carcass of them will wash his clothes, and be unclean until the even; they are unclean to you.
11:29 And these are they which are unclean to you among the swarming things that swarm on the earth: the weasel, and the mouse, and the great lizard after its kinds,
11:30 and the gecko, and the land-crocodile, and the lizard, and the sand-lizard, and the chameleon.
11:31 These are they which are unclean to you among all that swarm; whoever touches them, when they are dead, will be unclean until the even.
11:32 And on whatever any of them, when they are dead, falls, it will be unclean; whether it be any vessel of wood, or clothes, or skin, or sack, whatever vessel it be, with which any work is done, it must be put into water, and it will be unclean until the even; then will it be clean.
11:33 And every earthen vessel whereinto any of them falls, whatever is in it will be unclean, and it you shall break.
11:34 All food in it which may be eaten, that on which water comes, will be unclean; and all drink in every such vessel that may be drunk will be unclean.
11:35 And every thing after which any part of their carcass falls will be unclean; whether oven, or range for pots, it will be broken in pieces; they are unclean, and will be unclean to you.
11:36 Nevertheless a fountain or a cistern in which is a gathering of water will be clean; but he who touches their carcass will be unclean.
11:37 And if anything of their carcass fall on any sowing seed which is to be sown, it is clean.
11:38 But if water be put on the seed, and anything of their carcass fall on it, it is unclean to you.
11:39 And if any beast, of which you may eat, die, he that touches the carcass of it will be unclean until the even.
11:40 And he that eats of the carcass of it will wash his clothes, and be unclean until the even; he also that bears the carcass of it will wash his clothes, and be unclean until the even.
11:41 And every swarming thing that swarms on the earth is a detestable thing; it will not be eaten.
11:42 Whatever goes on the belly, and whatever goes on all fours, or whatever has many feet, even all swarming things that swarm on the earth, them you shall not eat; for they are a detestable thing.
11:43 You shall not make yourselves detestable with any swarming thing that swarms, neither shall you make yourselves unclean with them, that you should be defiled by it.
11:44 For I am HaShem your G-d; sanctify yourselves therefore, and be holy; for I am holy; neither shall you defile yourselves with any manner of swarming thing that moves on the earth.
11:45 For I am HaShem that brought you up out of the land of Egypt, to be your G-d; you shall therefore be holy, for I am holy.
11:46 This is the law of the beast, and of the fowl, and of every living creature that moves in the waters, and of every creature that swarms on the earth;
11:47 to make a difference between the unclean and the clean, and between the living thing that may be eaten and the living thing that may not be eaten.

13:1 And HaShem spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying:
13:2 When a man will have in the skin of his flesh a rising, or a scab, or a bright spot, and it become in the skin of his flesh the plague of leprosy, then he will be brought to Aaron the priest, or to one of his sons the priests.
13:3 And the priest will look on the plague in the skin of the flesh; and if the hair in the plague be turned white, and the appearance of the plague be deeper than the skin of his flesh, it is the plague of leprosy; and the priest will look on him, and pronounce him unclean.
13:4 And if the bright spot be white in the skin of his flesh, and the appearance of it be not deeper than the skin, and the hair of it be not turned white, then the priest will shut up him that has the plague seven days.
13:5 And the priest will look on him the seventh day; and if the plague stay in its appearance, and the plague be not spread in the skin, then the priest will shut him up seven days more.
13:6 And the priest will look on him again the seventh day; and if the plague be dim, and the plague be not spread in the skin, then the priest will pronounce him clean: it is a scab; and he will wash his clothes, and be clean.
13:7 But if the scab spread abroad in the skin, after that he has shown himself to the priest for his cleansing, he will show himself to the priest again.
13:8 And the priest will look, and if the scab be spread in the skin, then the priest will pronounce him unclean: it is leprosy.
13:9 When the plague of leprosy is in a man, then he will be brought to the priest.
13:10 And the priest will look, and if there be a white rising in the skin, and it have turned the hair white, and there be quick raw flesh in the rising,
13:11 it is an old leprosy in the skin of his flesh, and the priest will pronounce him unclean; he will not shut him up; for he is unclean.
13:12 And if the leprosy break out abroad in the skin, and the leprosy cover all the skin of him that has the plague from his head even to his feet, as far as appears to the priest;
13:13 then the priest will look; and if the leprosy have covered all his flesh, he will pronounce him clean that has the plague; it is all turned white: he is clean.
13:14 But whensoever raw flesh appears in him, he will be unclean.
13:15 And the priest will look on the raw flesh, and pronounce him unclean; the raw flesh is unclean: it is leprosy.
13:16 But if the raw flesh again be turned into white, then he will come to the priest;
13:17 and the priest will look on him; and if the plague be turned into white, then the priest will pronounce him clean that has the plague: he is clean.
13:18 And when the flesh has in the skin of it a boil, and it is healed,
13:19 and in the place of the boil there is a white rising, or a bright spot, reddish-white, then it will be shown to the priest.
13:20 And the priest will look; and if the appearance of it be lower than the skin, and the hair of it be turned white, then the priest will pronounce him unclean: it is the plague of leprosy, it has broken out in the boil.
13:21 But if the priest look on it, and there be no white hairs in it, and it be not lower than the skin, but be dim, then the priest will shut him up seven days.
13:22 And if it spread abroad in the skin, then the priest will pronounce him unclean: it is a plague.
13:23 But if the bright spot stay in its place, and be not spread, it is the scar of the boil; and the priest will pronounce him clean.
13:24 Or when the flesh has in the skin of it a burning by fire, and the quick flesh of the burning become a bright spot, reddish-white, or white;
13:25 then the priest will look on it; and if the hair in the bright spot be turned white, and the appearance of it be deeper than the skin, it is leprosy, it has broken out in the burning; and the priest will pronounce him unclean: it is the plague of leprosy.
13:26 But if the priest look on it, and there be no white hair in the bright spot, and it be no lower than the skin, but be dim; then the priest will shut him up seven days.
13:27 And the priest will look on him the seventh day; if it spread abroad in the skin, then the priest will pronounce him unclean: it is the plague of leprosy.
13:28 And if the bright spot stay in its place, and be not spread in the skin, but be dim, it is the rising of the burning, and the priest will pronounce him clean; for it is the scar of the burning.
13:29 And when a man or woman has a plague on the head or on the beard,
13:30 then the priest will look on the plague; and if the appearance of it be deeper than the skin, and there be in it yellow thin hair, then the priest will pronounce him unclean: it is a scall, it is leprosy of the head or of the beard.

13:32 And in the seventh day the priest will look on the plague; and if the scall be not spread, and there be in it no yellow hair, and the appearance of the scall be not deeper than the skin,
13:33 then he will be shaven, but the scall will he not shave; and the priest will shut up him that has the scall seven days more.
13:34 And in the seventh day the priest will look on the scall; and if the scall be not spread in the skin, and the appearance of it be not deeper than the skin, then the priest will pronounce him clean; and he will wash his clothes, and be clean.
13:35 But if the scall spread abroad in the skin after his cleansing,
13:36 then the priest will look on him; and if the scall be spread in the skin, the priest will not seek for the yellow hair: he is unclean.
13:37 But if the scall stay in its appearance, and black hair have grown up in it; the scall is healed, he is clean; and the priest will pronounce him clean.
13:38 And if a man or a woman have in the skin of their flesh bright spots, even white bright spots;
13:39 then the priest will look; and if the bright spots in the skin of their flesh be of a dull white, it is a tetter, it has broken out in the skin: he is clean.
13:40 And if a man's hair have fallen off his head, he is bald; yet is he clean.
13:41 And if his hair have fallen off from the front part of his head, he is forehead-bald; yet is he clean.
13:42 But if there be in the bald head, or the bald forehead, a reddish-white plague, it is leprosy breaking out in his bald head, or his bald forehead.
13:43 Then the priest will look on him; and if the rising of the plague be reddish-white in his bald head, or in his bald forehead, as the appearance of leprosy in the skin of the flesh,
13:44 he is a leprous man, he is unclean; the priest will surely pronounce him unclean: his plague is in his head.
13:45 And the leper in whom the plague is, his clothes will be rent, and the hair of his head will go loose, and he will cover his upper lip, and will cry: 'Unclean, unclean.'
13:46 All the days in which the plague is in him he will be unclean; he is unclean; he will live alone; without the camp will his dwelling be.
13:47 And when the plague of leprosy is in a garment, whether it be a woolen garment, or a linen garment;
13:48 or in the warp, or in the woof, whether they be of linen, or of wool; or in a skin, or in any thing made of skin.
13:49 If the plague be greenish or reddish in the garment, or in the skin, or in the warp, or in the woof, or in any thing of skin, it is the plague of leprosy, and will be shown to the priest.
13:50 And the priest will look on the plague, and shut up that which has the plague seven days.
13:51 And he will look on the plague on the seventh day: if the plague be spread in the garment, or in the warp, or in the woof, or in the skin, whatever service skin is used for, the plague is a malignant leprosy: it is unclean.
13:52 And he will burn the garment, or the warp, or the woof, whether it be of wool or of linen, or any thing of skin, in which the plague is; for it is a malignant leprosy; it will be burnt in the fire.
13:53 And if the priest will look, and the plague be not spread in the garment, or in the warp, or in the woof, or in any thing of skin;
13:54 then the priest will command that they wash the thing in which the plague is, and he will shut it up seven days more.
13:55 And the priest will look, after that the plague is washed; and if the plague have not changed its color, and the plague be not spread, it is unclean; you shall burn it in the fire; it is a fret, whether the bareness be within or without.
13:56 And if the priest look, and the plague be dim after the washing of it, then he will rend it out of the garment, or out of the skin, or out of the warp, or out of the woof.
13:57 And if it appear still in the garment, or in the warp, or in the woof, or in any thing of skin, it is breaking out, you shall burn that in which the plague is with fire.
13:58 And the garment, or the warp, or the woof, or whatever thing of skin it be, which you shall wash, if the plague have departed from them, then it will be washed the second time, and will be clean.
13:59 This is the law of the plague of leprosy in a garment of wool or linen, or in the warp, or in the woof, or in any thing of skin, to pronounce it clean, or to pronounce it unclean.

26:15 and if you shall reject My statutes, and if your soul abhor My ordinances, so that you will not do all My commandments, but break My covenant;
26:16 I also will do this to you: I will appoint terror over you, even consumption and fever, that will make the eyes to fail, and the soul to languish; and you shall sow your seed in vain, for your enemies will eat it.
26:17 And I will set My face against you, and you shall be struck before your enemies; they that hate you shall rule over you; and you shall flee when none pursues you.
26:18 And if you will not yet for these things listen to Me, then I will chastise you seven times more for your sins.
26:19 And I will break the pride of your power; and I will make your heaven as iron, and your earth as brass.
26:20 And your strength will be spent in vain; for your land will not yield her produce, neither will the trees of the land yield their fruit.
26:21 And if you walk contrary to Me, and will not listen to Me; I will bring seven times more plagues on you according to your sins.
26:22 And I will send the beast of the field among you, which will rob you of your children, and destroy your cattle, and make yourselves few in number; and your ways will become desolate.
26:23 And if in spite of these things you will not be corrected to Me, but will walk contrary to Me;
26:24 then will I also walk contrary to you; and I will strike you, even I, seven times for your sins.
26:25 And I will bring a sword on you, that will execute the vengeance of the covenant; and you shall be gathered together within your cities; and I will send the pestilence among you; and you shall be delivered into the hand of the enemy.
26:26 When I break your staff of bread, ten women will bake your bread in one oven, and they will deliver your bread again by weight; and you shall eat, and not be satisfied.
26:27 And if you will not for all this listen to Me, but walk contrary to Me;
26:28 then I will walk contrary to you in fury; and I also will chastise you seven times for your sins.
26:29 And you shall eat the flesh of your sons, and the flesh of your daughters shall you eat.
26:30 And I will destroy your high places, and cut down your sun-pillars, and cast your carcasses on the carcasses of your idols; and My soul will abhor you.
26:31 And I will make your cities a waste, and will bring your sanctuaries to desolation, and I will not smell the savor of your sweet odours.
26:32 And I will bring the land into desolation; and your enemies that live in it will be astonished at it.
26:33 And you will I scatter among the nations, and I will draw out the sword after you; and your land will be a desolation, and your cities will be a waste.
26:34 Then will the land be paid her sabbaths, as long as it lieth desolate, and you are in your enemies' land; even then will the land rest, and repay her sabbaths.
26:35 As long as it lieth desolate it will have rest; even the rest which it had not in your sabbaths, when you lived on it.
26:36 And as for them that are left of you, I will send a faintness into their heart in the lands of their enemies; and the sound of a driven leaf will chase them; and they will flee, as one flees from the sword; and they will fall when none pursues.
26:37 And they will stumble one on another, as it were before the sword, when none pursues; and you shall have no power to stand before your enemies.
26:38 And you shall perish among the nations, and the land of your enemies will eat you up.
26:39 And they that are left of you shall pine away in their iniquity in your enemies' lands; and also in the iniquities of their fathers will they pine away with them.
26:40 And they will confess their iniquity, and the iniquity of their fathers, in their treachery which they committed against Me, and also that they have walked contrary to Me.
26:41 I also will walk contrary to them, and bring them into the land of their enemies; if then perchance their uncircumcised heart be humbled, and they then be paid the punishment of their iniquity;
26:42 then will I remember My covenant with Jacob, and also My covenant with Isaac, and also My covenant with Abraham will I remember; and I will remember the land.
26:43 For the land will lie forsaken without them, and will be paid her sabbaths, while she lieth desolate without them; and they will be paid the punishment of their iniquity; because, even because they rejected My ordinances, and their soul abhorred My statutes.
26:44 And yet for all that, when they are in the land of their enemies, I will not reject them, neither will I abhor them, to destroy them utterly, and to break My covenant with them; for I am HaShem their G-d.
26:45 But I will for their sakes remember the covenant of their ancestors, whom I brought out of the land of Egypt in the sight of the nations, that I might be their G-d: I am HaShem.
26:46 These are the statutes and ordinances and laws, which HaShem made between Him and the children of Israel in mount Sinai by the hand of Moses.
27:1 And HaShem spoke to Moses, saying:
27:2 Speak to the children of Israel, and say to them: When a man will clearly utter a vow of persons to HaShem, according to your valuation,
27:3 then your valuation will be for the male from twenty years old even to sixty years old, even your valuation will be fifty shekels of silver, after the shekel of the sanctuary.
27:4 And if it be a female, then your valuation will be thirty shekels.
27:5 And if it be from five years old even to twenty years old, then your valuation will be for the male twenty shekels, and for the female ten shekels.
27:6 And if it be from a month old even to five years old, then your valuation will be for the male five shekels of silver, and for the female your valuation will be three shekels of silver.
27:7 And if it be from sixty years old and upward: if it be a male, then your valuation will be fifteen shekels, and for the female ten shekels.
27:8 But if he be too poor for your valuation, then he will be set before the priest, and the priest will value him; according to the means of him that vowed will the priest value him.
27:9 And if it be a beast, of which men bring an offering to HaShem, all that any man gives of such to HaShem will be holy.
27:10 He will not alter it, nor change it, a good for a bad, or a bad for a good; and if he will at all change beast for beast, then both it and that for which it is changed will be holy.
27:11 And if it be any unclean beast, of which they may not bring an offering to HaShem, then he will set the beast before the priest.
27:12 And the priest will value it, whether it be good or bad; as you the priest value it, so will it be.
27:13 But if he will indeed redeem it, then he will add the fifth part of it to your valuation.
27:14 And when a man will sanctify his house to be holy to HaShem, then the priest will value it, whether it be good or bad; as the priest will value it, so will it stand.
27:15 And if he that sanctified it will redeem his house, then he will add the fifth part of the money of your valuation to it, and it will be his.
27:16 And if a man will sanctify to HaShem part of the field of his possession, then your valuation will be according to the sowing of it; the sowing of a homer of barley will be valued at fifty shekels of silver.
27:17 If he sanctify his field from the year of jubilee, according to your valuation it will stand.
27:18 But if he sanctify his field after the jubilee, then the priest will reckon to him the money according to the years that remain to the year of jubilee, and an abatement will be made from your valuation.
27:19 And if he that sanctified the field will indeed redeem it, then he will add the fifth part of the money of your valuation to it, and it will be assured to him.
27:20 And if he will not redeem the field, or if he have sold the field to another man, it will not be redeemed any more.
27:21 But the field, when it goes out in the jubilee, will be holy to HaShem, as a field devoted; the possession of it will be the priest's.
27:22 And if he sanctify to HaShem a field which he has bought, which is not of the field of his possession;
27:23 then the priest will reckon to him the worth of your valuation to the year of jubilee; and he will give your valuation in that day, as a holy thing to HaShem.
27:24 In the year of jubilee the field will return to him of whom it was bought, even to him to whom the possession of the land belongs.
27:25 And all your valuations will be according to the shekel of the sanctuary; twenty gerahs will be the shekel.
27:26 However the firstling among beasts, which is born as a firstling to HaShem, no man will sanctify it; whether it be ox or sheep, it is HaShem's.
27:27 And if it be of an unclean beast, then he will ransom it according to your valuation, and will add to it the fifth part of it; or if it be not redeemed, then it will be sold according to your valuation.
27:28 Notwithstanding, no devoted thing, that a man may devote to HaShem of all that he has, whether of man or beast, or of the field of his possession, will be sold or redeemed; every devoted thing is most holy to HaShem.
27:29 None devoted, that may be devoted of men, will be ransomed; he will surely be put to death.
27:30 And all the tithe of the land, whether of the seed of the land, or of the fruit of the tree, is HaShem's; it is holy to HaShem.
27:31 And if a man will redeem anything of his tithe, he will add to it the fifth part of it.
27:32 And all the tithe of the herd or the flock, whatever passes under the rod, the tenth will be holy to HaShem.
27:33 He will not inquire whether it be good or bad, neither will he change it; and if he change it at all, then both it and that for which it is changed will be holy; it will not be redeemed.
27:34 These are the commandments, which HaShem commanded Moses for the children of Israel in mount Sinai.
