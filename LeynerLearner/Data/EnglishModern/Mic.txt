1:1 The word of HaShem that came to Micah the Morashtite in the days of Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah, which he saw concerning Samaria and Jerusalem.
1:2 Hear, you peoples, all of you; Listen, O earth, and all that in it is; and let the Lord HaShem be witness against you, the Lord from His holy temple.
1:3 For, HaShem comes out of His place, and will come down, and tread on the high places of the earth.
1:4 And the mountains will be molten under Him, and the valleys will be cleft, as wax before the fire, as waters that are poured down a steep place.
1:5 For the transgression of Jacob is all this, and for the sins of the house of Israel. What is the transgression of Jacob? is it not Samaria? And what are the high places of Judah? are they not Jerusalem?
1:6 Therefore I will make Samaria a heap in the field, a place for the planting of vineyards; and I will pour down the stones of it into the valley, and I will uncover the foundations of it.
1:7 And all her graven images will be beaten to pieces, and all her hires will be burned with fire, and all her idols will I lay desolate; for of the hire of a harlot has she gathered them, and to the hire of a harlot will they return.
1:8 For this will I wail and howl, I will go stripped and naked; I will make a wailing like the jackals, and a mourning like the ostriches.
1:9 For her wound is incurable; for it has come even to Judah; it reaches to the gate of my people, even to Jerusalem.
1:10 Do not tell it in Gath, do not weep at all; at Beth-le-aphrah roll yourself in the dust.
1:11 Pass away, O inhabitant of Saphir, in nakedness and shame; the inhabitant of Zaanan is not come out; the wailing of Beth-ezel will take from you the standing-place of it.
1:12 For the inhabitant of Maroth waits anxiously for good; because evil has come down from HaShem to the gate of Jerusalem.
1:13 Bind the chariots to the swift steeds, O inhabitant of Lachish; she was the beginning of sin to the daughter of Zion; for the transgressions of Israel are found in you.
1:14 Therefore shall you give a parting gift to Moresheth-gath; the houses of Achzib will be a deceitful thing to the kings of Israel.
1:15 I will yet bring to you, O inhabitant of Mareshah, him that will possess you; the glory of Israel will come even to Adullam.
1:16 Make yourself bald, and poll you for the children of your delight; enlarge your baldness as the vulture; for they have gone into captivity from you.
2:1 Woe to them that devise iniquity and work evil on their beds! When the morning is light, they execute it, because it is in the power of their hand.
2:2 And they covet fields, and seize them; and houses, and take them away; thus they oppress a man and his house, even a man and his heritage.
2:3 Therefore thus says HaShem: Look, against this family do I devise an evil, from which you shall not remove your necks, neither shall you walk upright; for it will be an evil time.
2:4 In that day will they take up a parable against you, and lament with a doleful lamentation, and say: 'We are utterly ruined; he changes the portion of my people; how does he remove it from me! Instead of restoring our fields, he divides them.'
2:5 Therefore you shall have none that will cast the line by lot in the congregation of HaShem.
2:6 'Preach you not', they preach; 'They will not preach of these things, that they will not take shame.'
2:7 Do I change, O house of Jacob? Is the spirit of HaShem straitened? Are these His doings? Do not My words do good to him that walks uprightly?
2:8 But of late My people has risen up as an enemy; with the garment you strip also the mantle from them that pass by securely, so that they are as men returning from war.
2:9 The women of My people you cast out from their pleasant houses; from their young children you take away My glory for ever.
2:10 Arise, and depart; for this is not your resting-place; because of the uncleanness of it, it will destroy you, even with a sore destruction.
2:11 If a man walking in wind and falsehood lies: 'I will preach to you of wine and of strong drink'; he will even be the preacher of this people.
2:12 I will surely assemble, O Jacob, all of you; I will surely gather the remnant of Israel; I will render them all as sheep in a fold; as a flock in the middle of their pasture; they will make great noise by reason of the multitude of men.
2:13 The breaker has gone up before them; they have broken out and passed on, by the gate, and have gone out thereat; and their king has passed on before them, and HaShem at the head of them.
3:1 And I said: Hear, please, you heads of Jacob, and rulers of the house of Israel: is it not for you to know justice?
3:2 Who hate the good, and love the evil; who rob their skin from off them, and their flesh from off their bones;
3:3 Who also eat the flesh of my people, and flay their skin from off them, and break their bones; indeed, they chop them in pieces, as that which is in the pot, and as flesh within the caldron.
3:4 Then will they cry to HaShem, but He will not answer them; indeed, He will hide His face from them at that time, according as they have wrought evil in their doings.
3:5 Thus says HaShem concerning the prophets that make my people to err; that cry: 'Peace', when their teeth have any thing to bite; and whoever does not put into their mouths, they even prepare war against him:
3:6 Therefore it will be night to you, that you shall have no vision; and it will be dark to you, that you shall not divine; and the sun will go down on the prophets, and the day will be black over them.
3:7 And the seers will be put to shame, and the diviners confounded; indeed, they will all cover their upper lips; for there will be no answer of G-d.
3:8 But I truly am full of power by the spirit of HaShem, and of justice, and of might, to declare to Jacob his transgression, and to Israel his sin.
3:9 Hear this, please, you heads of the house of Jacob, and rulers of the house of Israel, that abhor justice, and pervert all equity;
3:10 That build up Zion with blood, and Jerusalem with iniquity.
3:11 The heads of it judge for reward, and the priests of it teach for hire, and the prophets of it divine for money; yet will they lean on HaShem, and say: 'Is not HaShem in the middle of us? No evil will come on us'?
3:12 Therefore will Zion for your sake be plowed as a field, and Jerusalem will become heaps, and the mountain of the house as the high places of a forest.
4:1 But in the end of days it will happen, that the mountain of HaShem's house will be established as the top of the mountains, and it will be exalted above the hills; and peoples will flow to it.
4:2 And many nations will go and say: 'Come, and let us go up to the mountain of HaShem, and to the house of the G-d of Jacob; and He will teach us of His ways, and we will walk in His paths'; for out of Zion will go out the law, and the word of HaShem from Jerusalem.
4:3 And He will judge between many peoples, and will decide concerning mighty nations afar off; and they will beat their swords into plowshares, and their spears into pruninghooks; nation will not lift up sword against nation, neither will they learn war any more.
4:4 But they will sit every man under his vine and under his fig-tree; and none will make them afraid; for the mouth of HaShem of hosts has spoken.
4:5 For let all the peoples walk each one in the name of its god, but we will walk in the name of HaShem our G-d for ever and ever.
4:6 In that day, says HaShem, will I assemble her that halts, and I will gather her that is driven away, and her that I have afflicted;
4:7 And I will make her that halted a remnant, and her that was cast far off a mighty nation; and HaShem will reign over them in mount Zion from thenceforth even for ever.
4:8 And you, Migdal-eder, the hill of the daughter of Zion, to you will it come; indeed, the former dominion will come, the kingdom of the daughter of Jerusalem.
4:9 Now why do you cry out aloud? Is there no King in you, is your Counsellor perished, that pangs have taken hold of you as of a woman in travail?
4:10 Be in pain, and labor to bring out, O daughter of Zion, like a woman in travail; for now shall you go out of the city, and shall live in the field, and shall come even to Babylon; there shall you be rescued; there will HaShem redeem you from the hand of your enemies.
4:11 And now many nations are assembled against you, that say: 'Let her be defiled, and let our eye gaze on Zion.'
4:12 But they do not know the thoughts of HaShem, neither understand they His counsel; for He has gathered them as the sheaves to the threshing-floor.
4:13 Arise and thresh, O daughter of Zion; for I will make your horn iron, and I will make your hoofs brass; and you shall beat in pieces many peoples; and you shall devote their gain to HaShem, and their substance to the Lord of the whole earth.
4:14 Now shall you gather yourself in troops, O daughter of troops; they have laid siege against us; they strike the judge of Israel with a rod on the cheek.
5:1 But you, Beth-lehem Ephrathah, which art little to be among the thousands of Judah, out of you will one come out to Me that is to be ruler in Israel; whose goings out are from of old, from ancient days.
5:2 Therefore will He give them up, until the time that she who travails has brought out; then the residue of his brothers will return with the children of Israel.
5:3 And he will stand, and will feed his flock in the strength of HaShem, in the majesty of the name of HaShem his G-d; and they will abide, for then will he be great to the ends of the earth.
5:4 And this will be peace: when the Assyrian will come into our land, and when he will tread in our palaces, then should we raise against him seven shepherds, and eight princes among men.
5:5 And they will waste the land of Assyria with the sword, and the land of Nimrod with the keen-edged sword; and he will deliver us from the Assyrian, when he comes into our land, and when he treads within our border.
5:6 And the remnant of Jacob will be in the middle of many peoples, as dew from HaShem, as showers on the grass, that are not looked for from man, nor awaited at the hands of the sons of men.
5:7 And the remnant of Jacob will be among the nations, in the middle of many peoples, as a lion among the beasts of the forest, as a young lion among the flocks of sheep, who, if he go through, treads down and tears in pieces, and there is none to deliver.
5:8 Let Your hand be lifted up above Your adversaries, and let all Your enemies be cut off.
5:9 And it will happen in that day, says HaShem, that I will cut off your horses out of the middle of you, and will destroy your chariots;
5:10 And I will cut off the cities of your land, and will throw down all your strongholds;
5:11 And I will cut off witchcrafts out of your hand; and you shall have no more soothsayers;
5:12 And I will cut off your graven images and your pillars out of the middle of you; and you shall no more worship the work of your hands.
5:13 And I will pluck up your Asherim out of the middle of you; and I will destroy your enemies.
5:14 And I will execute vengeance in anger and fury on the nations, because they did not listen.
6:1 Hear now what HaShem says: Arise, contend before the mountains, and let the hills hear your voice.
6:2 Hear, O you mountains, HaShem's controversy, and you enduring rocks, the foundations of the earth; for HaShem has a controversy with His people, and He will plead with Israel.
6:3 O My people, what have I done to you? And in which have I wearied you? Testify against Me.
6:4 For I brought you up out of the land of Egypt, and redeemed you out of the house of bondage, and I sent before you Moses, Aaron, and Miriam.
6:5 O My people, remember now what Balak king of Moab devised, and what Balaam the son of Beor answered him; from Shittim to Gilgal, that you may know the righteous acts of HaShem.
6:6 'With which should I come before HaShem, and bow myself before G-d on high? Should I come before Him with burnt-offerings, with calves of a year old?
6:7 Will HaShem be pleased with thousands of rams, with ten thousands of rivers of oil? Should I give my first-born for my transgression, the fruit of my body for the sin of my soul?'
6:8 It has been told you, O man, what is good, and what HaShem requires of you: only to do justly, and to love mercy, and to walk humbly with your G-d.
6:9 Hark! HaShem crieth to the city — and it is wisdom to have regard for Your name — hear the rod, and who has appointed it.
6:10 Are there yet the treasures of wickedness in the house of the wicked, and the scant measure that is abominable?
6:11 'Should I be pure with wicked balances, and with a bag of deceitful weights?'
6:12 For the rich men of it are full of violence, and the inhabitants of it have spoken lies, and their tongue is deceitful in their mouth.
6:13 Therefore I also strike you with a grievous wound; I make you desolate because of your sins.
6:14 You shall eat, but not be satisfied; and your sickness will be in your inward parts; and you shall conceive, but shall not bring out; and whoever you bring out will I give up to the sword.
6:15 You shall sow, but shall not reap; you shall tread the olives, but shall not anoint you with oil; and the vintage, but shall not drink wine.
6:16 For the statutes of Omri are kept, and all the works of the house of Ahab, and you walk in their counsels; that I may make you an astonishment, and the inhabitants of it a hissing; and you shall bear the reproach of My people.
7:1 Woe is me! for I am as the last of the summer fruits, as the grape gleanings of the vintage; there is no cluster to eat; nor first-ripe fig which my soul desires.
7:2 The godly man is perished out of the earth, and the upright among men is no more; they all lie in wait for blood; they hunt every man his brother with a net.
7:3 Their hands are on that which is evil to do it diligently; the prince asks, and the judge is ready for a reward; and the great man, he utters the evil desire of his soul; thus they weave it together.
7:4 The best of them is as a brier; the most upright is worse than a thorn hedge; the day of your watchmen, even your visitation, has come; now will be their perplexity.
7:5 Do not trust in a friend, do not put confidence in a familiar friend; keep the doors of your mouth from her that lieth in your bosom.
7:6 For the son dishonours the father, the daughter rises up against her mother, the daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law; a man's enemies are the men of his own house.
7:7 'But as for me, I will look to HaShem; I will wait for the G-d of my salvation; my G-d will hear me.
7:8 Do not rejoice against me, O my enemy; though I have fallen, I will arise; though I sit in darkness, HaShem is a light to me.
7:9 I will bear the indignation of HaShem, because I have sinned against Him; until He plead my cause, and execute judgment for me; He will bring me out to the light, and I will see His righteousness.
7:10 Then my enemy will see it, and shame will cover her; who said to me: Where is HaShem your G-d? My eyes will gaze on her; now will she be trodden down as the mire of the streets.'
7:11 'The day for building your walls, even that day, will be far removed.'
7:12 There will be a day when they will come to you, from Assyria even to the cities of Egypt, and from Egypt even to the River, and from sea to sea, and from mountain to mountain.
7:13 And the land will be desolate for them that live in it, because of the fruit of their doings.
7:14 Tend Your people with Your staff, the flock of Your heritage, that live solitarily, as a forest in the middle of the fruitful field; let them feed in Bashan and Gilead, as in the days of old.
7:15 'As in the days of your coming out of the land of Egypt will I show to him marvellous things.'
7:16 The nations will see and be put to shame for all their might; they will lay their hand on their mouth, their ears will be deaf.
7:17 They will lick the dust like a serpent; like crawling things of the earth they will come trembling out of their close places; they will come with fear to HaShem our G-d, and will be afraid because of You.
7:18 Who is a G-d like to You, that pardons the iniquity, and passes by the transgression of the remnant of His heritage? He does not retain His anger for ever, because He delights in mercy.
7:19 He will again have compassion on us; He will subdue our iniquities; and You will cast all their sins into the depths of the sea.
7:20 You will show faithfulness to Jacob, mercy to Abraham, as You have sworn to our fathers from the days of old.
